DE LEGACY — New Community Initiative

DE LEGACY is launching a community initiative to create more opportunities and support the growth of our community: running and active programmes, park visits, school digital support and youth engagement.

Everything we build is designed with our community in mind.

Read the full message

We are excited to share that DE LEGACY is launching a new community initiative designed to create more opportunities and support the growth and development of our community.

This initiative will introduce a range of programmes and activities, including running and active programmes, community park visits, school digital support, youth engagement activities, community-based programmes and initiatives that encourage positive interaction between young men and women.

Through these programmes, DE aims to create spaces where people can connect, participate and access opportunities that support personal development, digital awareness and positive community engagement.

We invite our clients to get involved, support the initiative, partner with us and help us make a lasting difference.

More information on upcoming programmes, participation opportunities and launch activities will be announced across our official channels.

Thank you for your continued support.
